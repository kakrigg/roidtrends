Half Year Laboratory Summary

What's actually in the vial? Half Year Laboratory Testing Summary

January – June 2026 · IPED Trends Series

Half Year Laboratory Summaries report the results of independent laboratory analysis of image and performance enhancing drug products submitted by consumers and purchased from the unregulated market. Each product is tested for what compound it actually contains and at what concentration, then compared against the seller's label, giving a six-monthly picture of how reliably labels on the Australian IPED market reflect what is in the vial.

Download the Bulletin (PDF) Contact ROIDTrends

Key Findings

Key Takeaways

  • Only 12 of 26 products matched both the labelled compound and the labelled dose.
  • 6 of 20 steroid vials held a steroid not named on the label.
  • Three Retatrutide vials, all labelled 10 mg, ranged from 5.13 mg to 19 mg, one vial delivering close to four times another.

What we do

The ROIDTrends program raises awareness on IPED market trends in Australia

Monitor the market

Community members test products with PEDTest reagent kits and submit the results. Each submission records the drug, the formulation, what the label claimed and what the test found.

Survey in context

A short survey at the point of submission captures how people use IPEDs, what they do with test results, and how access to services changes over time, anonymously, linked across submissions.

Share what we find

Findings are published in rolling surveillance summaries and bulletins.
